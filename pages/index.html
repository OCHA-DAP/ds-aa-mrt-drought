Mauritania drought anticipatory action

Analyses and monitoring notes for the CERF-funded anticipatory-action framework for drought in Mauritania (endorsed April 2026). The framework triggers on a national ONM seasonal forecast (February–May) or on CHIRPS rainfall since 1 July (July–August), and funds activities in the moughataas of Wompou, Sélibaby and Ould Yengé in Guidimakha.

Analyses

How severe was the 2026 rainy season?

Rainfall, biomass, crop vegetation (FAO ASI), rangeland vegetation, surface water and temperature in 2026, each compared with every season since 2001 and with the framework's bad years, for the country, the southern belt, Guidimakha, and by wilaya and moughataa.
